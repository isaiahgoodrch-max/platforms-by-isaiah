Questions

What people ask before they book.

What does it cost?
One flat price, quoted up front after we talk. There is no number on this page because no two builds are the same size. Maintenance after launch is part of that price, not a second bill.
How long does it take?
You see your site on a call within days of the first conversation. Going live depends on one thing: how quickly you send me your photos, links and words. Most of the time the site is waiting on the client, not the other way round.
Do I own it?
It is yours. It lives at your own domain with your name on it, not on a platform that can change its terms or its prices, and nothing about it depends on a subscription staying active.
Do I need to know anything technical?
No. You never log in to anything. When something needs to change, you message me and I change it.
What happens when I want changes after launch?
That is what maintenance is. New photos, a new section, a price change, a fix. You send it, I ship it. No tickets and no hourly clock.
Is it a template?
No. Every site is designed and coded from scratch for the person whose name is on it, and nothing gets reused from one client to the next. That is why no two on this page look alike.
I run a business, not a channel. Is this for me?
Yes. The portfolio has a solar panel cleaning company, a trading course and coaching businesses sitting next to the creators. If your customers need to find you and trust you before they call, that is the job.
